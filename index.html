Key principles
Z2 wattage logic▼
Wed primary — target ~170W. Thu sits 5–8W lower — back-to-back days, conserve for Saturday. HR anchor: avg ~134, ceiling ~143 late. Drop watts if HR pushes 145+ before session ends. Z2 watts are trainer-only. Outdoor Strava watts are estimated and not comparable.
Outdoor HR vs power▼
No power meter outdoors — Strava watts read low (~130s–140s avg) due to coasting. Use HR as the primary effort signal. Target avg HR <150 on long rides where possible. Wind and elevation will naturally drive HR higher — context-adjust, don't panic.
Friday = sacred (W4–W8)▼
Nothing on Friday. No rides, no strength, no quick spin. As Saturday mileage climbs to 61–78 mi, arriving fresh matters. Non-negotiable in Weeks 4–8.
Travel weeks▼
Weeks 2, 3, and 6 constrained to ~44 mi. Trainer sessions are the compensatory lever — prioritize hitting full duration on Wed + Thu those weeks. Week 6 especially: longest trainer sessions of the build.
Strength during build▼
Keep Mon upper + Tue lower as-is. Do not progress loads aggressively in Weeks 4–8 — maintain, don't build.
Week 8 dress rehearsal▼
78 mi is the fitness test and race nutrition trial run. Full fuel protocol — Skratch 6 scoops per bottle, 4–5 sleeves Clif Bloks, target 60–90g carbs/hr (~250–275g total). Practice conservative pacing in miles 60–78. Report back to calibrate race day plan.
